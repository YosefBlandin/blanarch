Method note (applies to all sections). Google Play reviews were pulled on 2026-09-27 with the `google-play-scraper` library (English, US storefront, "newest" + "most relevant", about 1,070 unique reviews for FortiClient VPN and 225 for FortiClient EMS/ZTNA). App Store reviews were pulled from Apple's public customer-review RSS feed (us, gb, ca, au, in, ie, nz, sg storefronts; 58 unique reviews, only a few per storefront) plus the Apple "see all reviews" web pages. Individual Play/App Store reviews have no permalinks, so each quote cites the listing page it appears on, with date, star rating, app version and "helpful" count where available. Frequencies below are keyword tallies over 1-2 star reviews dated 2022 or later, plus my manual reading of all 1-3 star reviews longer than 50 characters (180 for FortiClient VPN Android, 38 for FortiClient Android, about 45 for iOS). They are rough and English-only.

## What do store reviews of FortiClient VPN and FortiClient (EMS/ZTNA) complain about most, and how do ratings look?

### Takeaway
Ratings are mediocre on Android (3.7 VPN, 3.3 EMS) and poor on iOS (2.2 VPN, 2.9 EMS). They are also polarised: about 23% of Android VPN ratings are 1 star. Among substantive negative reviews, the top complaints are, in order: (1) the connection drops or cannot be kept up, with no auto-reconnect; (2) it will not connect at all, often right after an app, OS or FortiGate update and often with no error message; (3) SSO/SAML problems, above all the hard requirement for Chrome on Android; (4) credentials, username or password not saved; (5) the MFA/token flow breaking when the user switches apps; (6) IPsec crypto options that are too limited since gateways moved from SSL VPN to IPsec; (7) an outdated, clunky UI. Battery, permissions and wiped profiles come up less often but still recur.

### Inferences
- Severity ranking for an employee: (1) drops without auto-reconnect or notice, (2) silent connect failures after updates, (3) Chrome-only SSO, (4) IPsec crypto mismatches. All four block work. Password and username saving, the lost-in-Recents MFA flow and the UI are high-frequency annoyances that add to the blockers because every drop forces a full re-login with OTP.
- Many "doesn't work" reviews are probably gateway-side mismatches (DH groups, IKE version, MDM policy). The app gives no actionable error ("no error msg", "three dots and then nothing"), so users blame the app. Clear error messages are a cheap differentiator.
- Frequency is skewed toward regressions: clusters line up with specific releases (7.0.6 in 2022, 7.2.1 in early 2024, the 7.2.3 username bug in June 2024, 7.4.5 on iOS in March 2025, 8.0.0 in mid-2026).

### Gaps
- Review scraping covered English/US on Play and only a handful of App Store storefronts (the RSS feed returned 0 US reviews for the VPN app, so US iOS VPN quotes come from Apple's web page, which shows only about 10). Spanish and other-language reviews were not analysed, and I found no reviews complaining about missing languages.
- There are no permalinks for individual store reviews, so quotes are cited to the listing page with dates.
- I found no Reddit threads specifically about the mobile apps. The Reddit searches returned nothing usable, so the Reddit perspective is missing. The iOS reviewer who says "confirmed across Reddit" is unverified.
- Frequencies are keyword or manual estimates, not statistically sampled. Play's "most relevant" sort biases toward helpful-voted reviews.
- I could not confirm "stuck at X%" and "-7200" as mobile store complaints. They appear in forum threads (for example the Android 15 thread stalling at about 60%, see below), not in the scraped store reviews.

## Which complaints are Android-specific (Android 12 to 16, VPN permission, background killing) vs iOS-specific?

### Takeaway
Android complaints centre on the OS killing or disconnecting the app when it goes to the background (the Android 12 wave in 2022), battery and "unrestricted battery" permission demands, Chrome-only SSO, and connect failures after major OS upgrades (Android 13, 14, 15), which are sometimes OEM-specific. iOS complaints centre on save-password never working (years-long), MFA prompts not appearing after app updates, iOS-release breakage (iOS 14.2, 16, 17, 18.3/18.4), and MDM "permission denied".

### Inferences
- For an Android client, the highest-value fixes users are asking for are: survive backgrounding (a foreground service plus correct VpnService use), auto-reconnect with a disconnect notification and a Disconnect/Reconnect action, SSO through any browser or Custom Tabs with the default browser, persisting credentials, keeping the app in Recents so users can copy an OTP, modern IPsec crypto if IPsec is supported, and precise error messages. These are inferences from the complaints above, not claims made by sources.
- Several Android failures are OEM or OS bugs (OnePlus Android 15) or gateway config (DH groups). Users still attribute them to the app, so diagnostics that separate "server rejected X" from "phone blocked Y" would reduce 1-star reviews.

### Gaps
- There were few direct complaints about Android's always-on VPN setting specifically ("No always on support", 2022, is the only explicit one found).
- I found no sourced complaints about missing languages, or about notification text other than the lack of a Disconnect action and of a disconnect alert.
- I did not quantify the iOS vs Android split rigorously because iOS review volume is small (about 220 ratings total in the US across both apps, vs about 29K on Android).
